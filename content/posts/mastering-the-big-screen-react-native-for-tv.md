TV app development has long been fragmented across platforms, each with its own tools and requirements, and there aren't many resources to help you along the way. That's why I'm happy to announce the release of **"Mastering the Big Screen - React Native for TV"**, a comprehensive guide that tackles these challenges head-on.

## Why This Book Matters

Demand for quality TV applications is higher than ever. Whether you're building a streaming service, a fitness platform, or an educational app, reaching users across different TV platforms has become essential.

## One Codebase, Multiple Platforms

Our book demonstrates how React Native enables you to deploy your application across different TV platforms.

## Real-World Expertise

Every chapter draws from real production experience rather than theory. We cover topics like:

- TV-specific UI/UX patterns
- Focus management
- Performance optimization
- Platform-specific considerations
- Accessibility
